How Attention Works in Long Reading Sessions

Sustained attention is a limited resource, and working memory holds only a few items at a time, so long articles quickly become tiring.

Why paragraphs matter

Short paragraphs give readers frequent resting points. Researchers have found that models of reading often treat a paragraph as a unit of meaning, and it’s important to keep each one focused on a single idea.

  • Chunking text into smaller sections reduces cognitive load for many readers.
    • Nested list items should be translated separately from their parents.
  • Clear headings act as signposts that help people regain their place after a distraction.
